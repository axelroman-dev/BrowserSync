Sync your bookmarks, history and passwords between your own devices through a server you host yourself. Everything is encrypted on your device before it's uploaded, so the server (and whoever runs it) only ever stores ciphertext.

Made for Chromium browsers without Chrome Sync (Brave, Vivaldi, Edge, Chromium, ...) and for anyone who'd rather not hand their data to Google.

WHAT'S NEW IN 2.2.0
  • A redesigned popup with four tabs: Sync, Passwords, Generator and Settings.
  • Passwords tab: the logins for the site you're on come first. Search, add and edit entries, and copy a username, password or two-step code in one click.
  • Settings tab: history, sync interval, website suggestions and autofill, plus Lock now and Log out.
  • A clearer Sync tab that shows when the last and next sync happen and what's being synced.
  • A redesigned password generator.
  • Your server's address is remembered, so you don't have to type it again after logging out.

FEATURES
  • Bookmarks: two-way sync with merge, so moves, renames and deletions carry over.
  • History: optional, with a configurable number of days to keep.
  • Password manager: suggestions and a save prompt on websites, optional autofill, a right-click menu, two-step verification codes (TOTP), and CSV import from Chrome, Firefox or Bitwarden.
  • Password generator: random characters or EFF word passphrases, with a strength meter.
  • Linked devices: see and sign out the devices using your account.
  • Encrypted backups you can export and restore.
  • English and Spanish.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
  • End-to-end encryption: your data key never leaves your devices unencrypted.
  • A recovery passphrase, shown once, lets you set up new devices or reset your password. Nobody else can recover your data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Open source.

REQUIREMENTS
You need your own BrowserSync server, version 2.0.0 or newer. It's free and open source, and runs with Docker. Setup guide: https://github.com/axelroman-dev/BrowserSync

Full release notes: https://github.com/axelroman-dev/BrowserSync/releases
